    What is fact here, and what is inference

    Fact: every stage below is built only from the official PlayStation descriptions — the chapter and mission names, the collectibles and their counts (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 52 areas, 8 garages) all come straight from that published text, quoted on each trophy's card.

    Missables — the honest answer: no official description states a one-chance window for any trophy, so this guide flags no confirmed missables. Two trophies rest on choices or a story-flavoured moment, and those two — and only those two — sit on an INFERRED watch-list, labelled as inference everywhere they appear. Treat the label as caution, not as an official fact.
